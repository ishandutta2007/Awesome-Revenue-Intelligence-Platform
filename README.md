## Top Revenue Intelligence Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Pipeline Visibility, AI Forecasting, Conversation Intelligence, Activity Capture, Deal Risk Scoring & Revenue Insights*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Revenue Intelligence**. These tools analyze CRM data, sales conversations, emails, and activity signals to deliver accurate forecasts, deal insights, coaching opportunities, and real-time revenue visibility for sales and RevOps teams.



**Examples** include Clari, Gong, People.ai, InsightSquared, BoostUp, Aviso, Revenue Grid, Scratchpad, Salesloft, 6sense, Salesken, Attention, Mediafly Revenue360, and Revenue.io (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise revenue intelligence platforms with conversation intelligence, automatic activity capture, and advanced AI forecasting remain predominantly commercial. Open-source strength exists in CRM foundations, workflow automation, data pipelines, analytics dashboards, and emerging AI/RevOps prototypes. This section is expanded with every relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Modern open-source CRM (Salesforce alternative) that provides a strong foundation for pipeline data and can be extended with custom intelligence layers.



- **[n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)**  

  Open-source workflow automation platform widely used to build custom revenue intelligence pipelines (CRM sync, AI analysis of calls/emails, deal risk alerts, and Slack/Notion updates).



- **[Airbyte + dbt](https://github.com/airbytehq/airbyte)**  

  Open-source data integration and transformation stack that powers many modern RevOps and revenue intelligence data platforms.



- **[Metabase / Apache Superset](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  

  Open-source BI and analytics tools commonly used for pipeline dashboards, forecast reporting, win/loss analysis, and revenue insights.



- **[Odoo / ERPNext / SuiteCRM](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Full-featured open-source CRM and ERP systems that support sales pipelines and can serve as the system of record for custom intelligence solutions.



- **[Custom AI RevOps & deal intelligence engines](https://github.com/search?q=revenue+intelligence+OR+deal+risk+OR+RevOps+AI)**  

  Community projects that combine CRM data, conversation transcripts, and LLMs to flag deal risk, extract insights, and automate coaching signals.



- **[Relaticle and other modern open CRMs](https://github.com/relaticle/relaticle)**  

  Newer open-source CRMs with AI agent support and extensible data models suitable for revenue workflows.



- **[Lead-to-cash and pipeline automation projects](https://github.com/search?q=lead-to-cash+OR+sales+pipeline+automation)**  

  Open-source implementations of end-to-end revenue lifecycle automation and basic forecasting logic.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Conversation & transcript analysis**: Open libraries and Whisper-based pipelines for processing sales call recordings.

- **Activity capture helpers**: Scripts and integrations that parse email/calendar data into structured CRM activity.

- **Forecasting models**: Python notebooks using Prophet, scikit-learn, or modern ML for pipeline and revenue prediction.

- **Reverse ETL & activation**: Tools that push intelligence scores and insights back into the CRM.

- **Knowledge systems**: Obsidian or similar structured vaults adapted as GTM/RevOps systems of record.

- Workflow orchestration (Temporal, Prefect, Dagster) for reliable intelligence pipelines.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Combine an open-source CRM (**Twenty**, **Odoo**, or **SuiteCRM**) as the system of record with **n8n** for automation, **Airbyte + dbt** for data unification, and **Metabase/Superset** for dashboards.  

Layer LLM-based analysis for conversation intelligence and deal risk scoring.  

Commercial platforms (Clari, Gong, People.ai, BoostUp, Aviso, etc.) deliver production-grade conversation intelligence, automatic activity capture, enterprise forecasting accuracy, and coaching insights that require significant engineering effort to approximate with open-source components.  

Many teams run hybrid stacks: open-source data and workflow layers underneath specialized commercial revenue intelligence tools.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Revenue intelligence platforms influence forecasting, coaching, compensation, and go-to-market decisions. Data accuracy, privacy (especially call recordings and email content), and security are critical.

- Open-source solutions provide transparency and flexibility but demand ongoing engineering investment for integrations, model quality, scalability, and compliance. They are rarely complete drop-in replacements for mature commercial revenue intelligence platforms in large organizations.
